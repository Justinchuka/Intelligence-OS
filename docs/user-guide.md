# User guide

## 1. First session (onboarding)
1. Say "Set up an Intelligence OS".
2. Tell it who the OS is for: you, a business or brand, a team, or a project.
3. Name it, or skip. If you skip and it already knows a name from you, it will suggest one and tell you it was auto-suggested.
4. Pick one of three starting looks: Essential — Minimal, Intelligence — Operations, Atmosphere — Glass. Seven more appear later.
5. Answer a few everyday questions ("What are you trying to make progress on?"). You do not need goals or numbers.
6. Review the proposal table: goals, modules, dashboard sections. For each: accept, edit, reject or defer. Nothing starts until you approve.
7. Download the `os-state` file it gives you. Upload it next time.

Prefer to browse? Ask for the **catalogue route**: 10 categories, 10 items each. You can have up to 15 active custom features.

## 2. Everyday use
| I want to | Say |
|---|---|
| Log something | "I walked 6,200 steps today." It asks one question if the unit or tracker is unclear. |
| Find opportunities | "Run my Job Finder (standard)." Needs web search. |
| See patterns | "Analyse my expenses for the last 30 days." |
| Turn a finding into a task | "Make an action from that scholarship." Suggested actions wait for your approval. |
| Change look | "Switch to Observatory — Data." Your data is unchanged. |
| Turn a feature off | "Disable Habit Tracker" (stops it) or "Hide it" (just invisible). |
| Undo a setup change | "Show config versions" then "Roll back to version 3." Records are not rolled back. |
| Share safely | "Export a public config" (removes your profile and sensitive trackers). |

## 3. Reading results
- **Confidence labels** (Verified to Unverified) come with a reason. They are not probabilities.
- **Counts**: source checks, queries, results, unique records, new findings and updates are separate numbers.
- **Analysis**: Observation (what the data shows), Interpretation, Recommendation are labelled. Gaps are shown, not filled.

## 4. Keeping your data
Download the updated state pack at the end of each session. It is your database. Exports (CSV/Excel) are for reading and sharing; they are not the master copy.

## 5. Moving to a standalone app later
Export the config package. A real standalone product needs a backend, database, authentication, storage, integrations, secrets management, scheduler and AI-provider setup; none of that is in this Skill. See `references/architecture-capability-boundary.md`.
